Copy direction
The current copy is informative but long and sometimes repetitive. A good rewrite should move toward:
shorter headlines
clearer benefit-first language
technical detail below the fold
fewer paragraphs, more structured bullets/spec blocks
stronger conversion language
Example tone:
“Engineered for chloramine reduction”
“Built for consistent adsorption performance”
“Reliable supply from large-scale coconut shell carbon manufacturing”
“Application-matched grades for water, gas, and industrial treatment”
That keeps the technical seriousness while sounding current.
What to keep from the original content
Keep these facts visible in the redesign because they are strong trust anchors from the current site:
Coconut activated carbon focus
NSF 42 / NSF 61 references
Prop 65 compliant messaging
Product family breakdown
Application sectors
Established 2010 / 2 production centers / 35000 metric tons
Pittsburgh HQ and contact details
